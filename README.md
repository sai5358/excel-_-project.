# 📊 Vrinda Store Sales Analysis | Microsoft Excel

## 📖 Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end sales analysis of the **Vrinda Store** dataset using **Microsoft Excel**. The objective was to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, processing, analysis, and interactive dashboard development.

By analyzing customer demographics, sales performance, order channels, and regional trends, this project demonstrates how Excel can be leveraged as a Business Intelligence tool to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Vrinda Store aims to better understand customer purchasing behavior and identify the key factors influencing sales performance. The goal is to uncover actionable insights that can help optimize marketing strategies, improve customer targeting, and drive business growth.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing patterns across different demographics.
- Identify the highest-performing sales channels.
- Determine top-performing states and customer segments.
- Develop an interactive dashboard for business reporting.
- Generate actionable recommendations to improve sales performance.

## 📈 Key Business Insights

- Women accounted for approximately **65%** of total purchases, making them the primary customer segment.
- Customers aged **30–49 years** contributed nearly **50%** of overall sales, representing the most valuable age group.
- **Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra** collectively generated approximately **80%** of total sales, highlighting the importance of online marketplaces.
- **Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh** emerged as the top-performing states based on revenue contribution.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following strategies are recommended:

- Focus marketing campaigns on women aged **30–49 years**.
- Strengthen partnerships and promotional campaigns across Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra.
- Increase targeted marketing efforts in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh.
- Launch personalized offers and customer loyalty programs to improve retention and repeat purchases.
